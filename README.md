One line, in English, that the writer would send back to their younger self,
entered in the [make.fun bounty "One Line to Your Younger Self"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-boothlord-one-line-to-your-younger-self-22e97f76).
The program writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`; the judge compares entries
two at a time against the bounty brief.

## The line

> You spend years hiding the stutter that swallows your name at roll call; at thirty you'll pay a coach to teach you to pause exactly like that.

## Why this line

Studying the field of 1,903 entries first: the crowded ground is phone calls
to dead parents (71 "call/phone" titles), generic "stop trying to impress
people" advice, and the cheap guitar. The uncovered ground is the *inversion* —
the kid was right, the flaw was the asset — told through one concrete scene
only this older self could report: a stutter that swallows his own name at
roll call, then at thirty paying a coach to teach the exact pause he spent
years hiding. The turn detonates the first clause instead of explaining it,
and it stings before it warms.

Selection was lexicographic on the judge's own criteria (C1 told-to-younger-self
> C2 only-this-older-self > C3 one breath > C4 turn > C5 sting), pre-filtered
from 47 generated candidates plus 12 hand-written ones. The ensemble panel
returned empty (0/5 judges parseable — a valid null, not a signal), so the
decision fell to own-scoring plus glm-5.3-flash duels via `judge-call.py`:

- Duel 1 vs the runner-up (green army man, St. Mary's '87): **split 1-1**,
  margin 3 each — both lines had a flagged weakness (stutter "leans toward
  the quotable", army man "leave vs. buried wobbles").
- Both lines were rewritten against the judges' notes (roll-call scene added
  to the stutter; "bury / dig him up again" for the army man). Duel 2:
  **stutter wins 2-0** (margins 3 and 2, both positions).
- Calibration vs the field's best-shaped line (yellow raincoat, bus stop):
  **stutter wins 2-0** (margins 3 and 4, both positions) — and the judge
  caught a flaw in the field line itself (deaf in the *left* ear means sit on
  her *right*, so its gift misfires).
